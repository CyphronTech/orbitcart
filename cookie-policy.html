Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Cookies and similar technologies are small items stored in your browser. This site uses very few. This page lists each one so you can decide.

What we use

Name / providerPurposeTypeDuration
cyphron_consent_v1 (this site, browser storage)Remembers your cookie choice so we do not keep askingStrictly necessaryUp to 12 months
Google reCAPTCHA via Firebase App Check (Google LLC)Spam and abuse protection when you send a form. Google may set a cookie such as _GRECAPTCHA on google.com and App Check may keep a token in your browser's IndexedDBOptional: loaded only if you allow it, and only when you send a formUp to 6 months

We do not use analytics, advertising, social-media or tracking cookies, and we do not load third-party fonts or tracking scripts.

Your choices

  • When you first visit, a small notice lets you accept or decline the optional item with equal ease.
  • Change your mind at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page.
  • You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.

If you reject the optional item you can still contact us: tick the optional spam-protection box on a form if you change your mind, use the email draft the form offers, or email or phone us directly. Nothing else on the site depends on it.

Google's role

If you allow it, Google reCAPTCHA analyses the interaction with the form to tell people from bots. Google's Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) and Terms of Service (opens in a new tab) apply to that check.
