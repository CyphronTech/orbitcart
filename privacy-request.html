Privacy and data requests

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to withdraw a consent you gave. Use the form below or email us. Both are treated the same.

  • We aim to respond within 30 days. There is no fee for reasonable requests.
  • Deleting your data does not affect records we must keep by law, such as tax records for paid projects.
